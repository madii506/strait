06 — Questions

The obvious questions.

Who holds the money?

Nobody. Your wallet signs one transaction that swaps your coin and delivers USDC to their wallet. There is no Strait account and no Strait vault, so there is nothing for us to hold, freeze or lose.

What does it cost?

Strait takes no fee. You pay the Solana network fee and the swap's price impact, both shown before you sign. If their wallet has never held USDC, you also pay about 0.002 SOL to open their USDC account.

Which coins can I pay with?

Anything the router can take to USDC: SOL, the majors and most memecoins with real liquidity. If a coin has no route, the page tells you before you sign anything.

Do they always get the exact amount?

When the route supports it, yes: the exact amount, to the cent. On thinner coins the page quotes "at least" the amount instead and says so before you pay; they may get up to about 1% more.

What does the person I'm paying need?

A Solana wallet address. The page checks it is a wallet and not a token account before letting you pay.

Can a payment be reversed?

No. A confirmed Solana transaction is final, which is why there are no chargebacks. Check the address before you approve.

What is $STRAIT?

The project's coin. You never need it to pay or to get paid. Its contract address will appear in the bar at the top of this site at launch and nowhere else first.

Who runs this, and what can go wrong?

An independent team. The site asks Jupiter's router for a route, builds the transaction and hands it to your wallet; your wallet shows exactly what it does before you approve. If the price moves past the slippage limit, the transaction fails and nothing moves.

Strait is not affiliated with Solana, Jupiter, Phantom or Circle.
