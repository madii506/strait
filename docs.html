How Strait works

2 min read
01 / Overview

Any coin in. Dollars out.

Strait lets you pay anyone on Solana with whatever you hold. You pick a coin, the person you pay receives USDC, and both happen in one transaction that your wallet signs. There is no Strait account, no vault and no escrow, so there is nothing for anyone to hold in between.

Note 1The most important thing first: a confirmed payment cannot be reversed by anyone, including us. That is what makes it instant and chargeback-free, and it is why the pay page checks the address before you can pay.
02 / Paying

Paying someone

  1. Open Pay, or a request link someone sent you.
  2. Enter their wallet address and an amount in dollars, if the link didn't already.
  3. Connect Phantom, Solflare or Backpack. Your coins appear with their dollar values.
  4. Pick a coin. The quote shows what you spend, what they receive, the route and the price impact.
  5. Press Pay and approve once. The page shows each step until the payment is settled.

On a phone without a wallet extension, the pay page offers to open itself inside Phantom or Solflare.

03 / Requesting

Getting paid

On the home page, under Request, enter your wallet, an amount and an optional note. You get a link and a QR code. The link is plain:

/pay?to=<your wallet>&usd=50&note=dinner

Whoever opens it can pay with any coin they hold. You receive USDC. Nothing is stored on our side; the link carries everything.

04 / One transaction

What the transaction contains

StepWhat it does
Compute budgetSets the compute limit and priority fee, from Jupiter's estimate plus headroom for the steps below.
Open their USDC accountCreateIdempotent on the associated token account program. Does nothing if it already exists; costs about 0.002 SOL if it doesn't.
SwapJupiter's route from your coin to USDC, with the output going straight to their USDC account.
Memostrait:v1 plus your note, written on chain.
ReferenceA zero-lamport transfer to yourself that carries the read-only reference key, so the payment can be found.

When you pay with USDC itself there is no swap: the transaction is a direct TransferChecked to their USDC account, with the same memo and reference.

Before your wallet sees it, the page simulates the transaction on Solana. If the simulation fails, you are never asked to sign.

05 / Exact amounts

Exact, or at least

Strait first asks the router for an exact-out quote: they receive exactly the amount, and you spend at most the quoted input plus 1% slippage. Some routes can't be quoted exact-out. Then Strait sizes an exact-in swap so that even the worst allowed fill covers the amount, and the page says "at least". In that case they may receive up to about 1% more.

06 / Proof

Proof and the reference key

Every Strait payment carries this read-only key:

The Proof section on the home page calls getSignaturesForAddress on that key, reads each transaction, and keeps only the ones with the strait:v1 memo. For each one it shows the payer, the recipient, the USDC that arrived and the coin that paid for it. Nothing is stored by us; the list is rebuilt from the chain on every read.

07 / Fees and limits

Fees and limits

Strait feeNone
Network feeSolana's base fee plus a capped priority fee (at most 0.001 SOL)
Their USDC accountAbout 0.002 SOL, only if they have never held USDC
Per payment$0.50 to $10,000
Slippage limit1%

$STRAIT is the project's coin. You never need it to pay or get paid.

08 / Endpoints

Endpoints

The site talks to three small endpoints. You can call them yourself.

EndpointReturns
GET /api/jup?op=quoteA Jupiter quote from a coin to USDC. Here: $10 paid in SOL, exact-out.
GET /api/jup?op=tokensToken metadata and price, by mint or symbol.
GET /api/proofThe latest Strait payments, rebuilt from the chain.
POST /api/rpcAn allow-listed Solana RPC relay (balances, token accounts, blockhash, simulate, send, status).
09 / What can go wrong

What can go wrong

CaseOutcome
Your coin has no route to USDCThe quote says so. You are never asked to sign.
The price moves more than 1% while you approveThe transaction fails on chain. Nothing moves except the network fee.
You paste a token account or a programThe page refuses it and asks for a wallet address.
You pay the wrong walletFinal. Nobody can reverse it. Check the address.
The quote is staleQuotes refresh every 20 seconds and again right before you sign.
